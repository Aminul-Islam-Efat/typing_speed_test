About NeonType

NeonType is a free, browser-based typing speed test built for anyone who wants to measure and improve their typing speed and accuracy. Whether you are a student preparing for an exam, a programmer practising code typing, or a professional who wants to type faster — NeonType gives you a distraction-free space to practise.

Our Mission

We believe that improving your typing speed should not require an account, a download, or a paid subscription. Everything on NeonType runs entirely in your browser — your keystrokes never leave your device.

Key Features

  • Test durations of 15, 30, 60 and 120 seconds
  • Three modes — words, sentences and real code snippets
  • Real-time WPM, accuracy, error counter and character count
  • Strict Mode that blocks wrong keystrokes so you build proper muscle memory
  • Mechanical keyboard sound feedback (optional)
  • Detailed results with a WPM-over-time chart

How We Make Money

NeonType is supported by non-intrusive advertising. Ads never appear inside the typing area, so your focus is never interrupted.
